## 说明 · Description

On phones (below 760 px) the module list was hidden with nothing in its place: no way to switch scale, save or open.

- A row of chips at the top of the parameters (00 London map, 01 growth, any tools), the current one highlighted; it stays at the top while the parameters scroll and slides sideways when there are more. ⋯ holds Save workspace, Open… and the build.
- The parameters fill the rest of the screen and scroll inside; short phones (height ≤ 700) start with 46% map instead of 52%.
- The map credits start folded to their ⓘ (unfolded they covered the scale bar).

Tested at iPhone SE 375×667, iPhone 16 Pro 402×874, Pro Max 440×956, Galaxy 360×780, Pixel 412×915 and 320×568: no sideways overflow, the row stays put while scrolling, the menu stays on screen. Landscape phones (956×440), iPad and computers are unchanged (side columns). `npm test` passes.
