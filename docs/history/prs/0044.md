## 说明 · Description

Step 6 turns the plan chosen in step 5 into stacked walkway plates built as one subdivision surface (engine/deck.js). Levels sit at 7.2 / 10.8 / 14.4 m; level 3 only around the main towers; every level-3 edge gets a ramp. Plate edges follow the step 5 corridor outline.

The page gets a step 6 card, the 3D view uses the new surface, and the 3D stage of the demo now belongs to step 6 so step 5 stays in plan.

The 3D view was not run in a browser here (three.js could not be loaded in the sandbox); engine and page wiring are covered by deck.test.mjs and a syntax check.
