## 说明 · Description

Nine flat ground layers sit 4-15 cm apart. With a 20 m near-plane cap the perspective depth buffer resolves only ~43 cm at 12 km, so layers swapped every frame.

- logarithmic depth buffer
- near plane follows distance (dist/40, max 300 m, under half the camera height)
- ground materials stop writing depth and draw in a fixed bottom-to-top order; columns now start at ground level
- the surroundings group (G.ctx3) was built but never added to the scene
