## 说明 · Description

The first time on a device (no language chosen yet) the app follows the device system language: any Chinese (zh-CN, zh-TW, …) → 中文, every other → English. Before, it always started in Chinese.

- Main app: `shared/i18n` (`systemLang()`; outside a browser, e.g. tests, it stays zh).
- The owner's apps when opened on their own: London map 3.43.6, Tensor Studio 8.47.1, VR v0.55, Structure Studio.
- A choice made with 中文 / EN is kept on the device and wins; apps opened from the main app still take its language.
- The friend's apps (Growth Simulator, Loop City) are unchanged.

Checked with system languages en-US, zh-CN, zh-TW, ja-JP and with a saved choice overriding the system (both ways). `npm test` and the map tests pass.
