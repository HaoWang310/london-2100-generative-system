## 说明 · Description

Two changes proven on the London generality test (branch `test/tensor-generality`, 10 typical sites), now made properly in Tensor Studio (8.48.0):

**Flood option B, the new default.** High-risk land (EA, >= 3.3 %/yr) is no longer cut out of the site: it becomes a blue-green corridor. No street runs or starts in it, no attractor is placed in it; a street crosses it within 30 degrees of square to the flow (downhill), up to 200 m, on a causeway. Causeways are drawn (pale double line), counted in the summary and the flood numbers, exported as a `causeway` layer, and not counted as streets in the flood by the score. Patches under 1.5 ha are not corridors. The previous keep-clear options remain in step 3. On the test, Barking went from 30 to 100 streets and 82 to 118 ha of reachable land.

**Keep only the large buildings.** "Keep, go round" becomes "Keep the large ones": buildings with a footprint of 1,500 m² or more stay; `?keep=all` keeps them all, as before. On the test, Soho went from 40 to 79 ha of land and a 1,157 ha site from 307 s to about 8 s to open.

**Fix.** A street cut back to a single point made the growth stop with an error (closeGen).

Checked: the station P2 example in both modes (181 streets, 8 causeways, no errors), and Barking, Soho and Hampstead from real map data with buildings kept and cleared. The Tensor Studio unit tests pass.
