## 说明 · Description

Opened from the catalogue on a slow connection, an app's own EN / 中文 switch stood in its panel while the page was still loading (seen on Loop City step 1 while "reading the base map").

**Cause.** The style that hides the switch was added at "reveal" and at `load` only. Reveal can fire (after 2.5 s) while the frame is still `about:blank`, and `load` waits for the app's pictures, so the switch showed until then.

**Change.** `app/appview.js`: the style and the language are given as soon as the app's page has a `<head>` (the same `followPages` already used for nested apps).

**Checked.** With the page delayed 4 s and its pictures 8 s: the switch was visible from 4.8 s to 15 s before, never after. All seven apps with a switch: hidden, follow to English and back. `node --test` 165/165, isolation OK.
