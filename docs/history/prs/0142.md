## 说明 · Description

The wool page (Loop City step 2), opened from the catalogue, now shows the example on step 1 at once, still. **▶ Demo** then works the steps out one by one in the browser: each step is computed (the bar says how long), grows on the map from station P2, and holds a moment. Step 10 is the wool relaxing live; 11 to 16 trace the roads. Stop any time; a step clicked is worked out up to there.

The computation is faster with **the same result**: a fingerprint of the example (data of steps 1–9, relaxed wool, roads and hierarchy of 11–16, a picture of every step in both looks) matches `main` exactly.
