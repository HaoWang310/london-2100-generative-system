## 说明 · Description

## What changes

The workspace's map (main app, **Display → Analysis layers**) now lists the same ten analysis layers, in the same five groups, as the incubator London map v3.44.1. It had four.

| Group | Layer | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain | EA LiDAR DTM 1 m | a square | cut to the 70 km circle |
| Terrain | Colour by height + hill shading | — | new |
| Terrain | Contours · OS Terrain 50 | — | new |
| Water | Flood risk, drainage network | yes | unchanged |
| Planning boundaries | London Green Belt, AONB | — | new |
| Transport | Rail by service, HS1 / HS2 with place names | — | new |
| Study area | 70 km study area | yes | unchanged |

## How

- Colour by height, Green Belt, AONB, rail and HS1 / HS2 are drawn by the incubator's own `scripts/planning_layers.js` and read its `assets/planning/` data. Their look, their cut to the circle and the HS place names are decided there, so a change made in the London map reaches the workspace.
- Contours use the incubator's contour worker (`map_core.js`) and its 50 m DEM; the line layer is wired in `shared/map-viewer/london-map.js`, as flood and drainage already are. On the dark theme the lines are a light sand so they still read.
- The EA LiDAR loader now gets the circle's centre and radius.
- Order as in the London map: terrain picture, contours, boundary fills, flood, drainage network, then boundary edges, rail and high-speed lines.

## Two fixes that came with it

- With no module overlay, analysis layers were painted **over** the 3D buildings (flood colours across the buildings in the 3D view). They now go under the 3D city, as in the London map.
- Long credits (several layers on) wrap beside the scale bar, not over it.
