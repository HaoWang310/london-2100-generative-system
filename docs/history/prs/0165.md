## What changes

**London map 3.45.0: 3D relief**, a new layer in the Terrain group, and the same switch in the workspace map.

- The ground itself rises by its height (MapLibre terrain on the open height tiles, about 30 m). Draped layers follow it (height colours, flood, boundaries, rail); the 3D buildings stand on it.
- Only inside the 70 km study circle: the heights go through the relief worker, which lays the ground flat outside the circle, with a 2.5 km slope at the edge instead of a wall.
- Exaggeration ×1 to ×8, ×3 at first (London is low). A flat map tilts once when the layer is switched on.
- Trees, street vehicles and building shadows are drawn on flat ground and would be buried or lost: they are switched off while the relief is on and come back, as they were, when it goes off.
- Workspace map: listed in the same place, drawn by the same incubator script; its Display rows for trees, vehicles and shadows are held the same way.

This is the last layer of `incubator/loop-city-real-map` that the London map did not have.
