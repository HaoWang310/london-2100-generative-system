Step 3 (`incubator/loop-city-tensor-base`, the base for the tensor field) stood between the wool bands and Tensor Studio only to read the parcel package as a site file. That reading moves into Tensor Studio, the wool hands its parcels straight on, and the page is deleted. The research line has six steps; the catalogue lists seven apps (VR is 07).

- The way back is the wool bands. The built-in P2 example and the Loop City example parcels are unchanged.

### Wool bands, step 17
- The main button is **Open in Tensor Studio**, and the chain row's Next does the same.
- Three checks before the hand-over, in the numbers on the right: across (up to 3.6 km), area (at least 2 ha), parcels touch. If one fails, the button and Next are grey and say why; a failing row stands at the top. Saving the package, GeoJSON and DXF still works.

- GeoJSON and DXF gain two layers: the site's edge (`SITE_EDGE`) and the anchors at the wool junctions (`ANCHOR_STUB`).
