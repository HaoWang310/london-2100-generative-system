## 说明 · Description

## What changes

One language for the whole site. The home page, the whole system and VR already keep it in one place (`localStorage` `l2100.lang`). The five incubator apps each kept their own, so:

- a language chosen in one app was not known to the next one opened (the wool in Chinese, then Next, and Tensor Studio or Structure Studio could come up in English);
- Loop City steps 1 and 2 opened in English when nothing was chosen, whatever the device's language.

### How
- **`shared/ui/lang.js`**, loaded as the first script of each app's page with the app's own key and language button (the same two values as its catalogue entry):
  1. on opening, the app's key is set to the shared language, so the app starts in it;
  2. a change made in the app is written back to the shared place;
  3. a change made in another tab presses the app's own button, so pages that are open follow at once.
- Nothing chosen anywhere: the device's language (Chinese for any Chinese, English for every other), the rule the home page already had.
- **`shared/i18n/index.js`**: the home page follows a change made in an app's own tab.
- **One line each** in the London map, Loop City steps 1 and 2, Tensor Studio (8.51.1) and Structure Studio. Their own language code is unchanged.

### Behaviour to know
- The first time, the home page's choice wins; if none was made there, the device's language. A choice made earlier inside a single app no longer counts.
- A `#lang=zh` address on steps 1 and 2 still sets that page's language, and now it is kept for the whole site, like a press of the switch.

## Checks
- `tools/tests/lang.test.mjs` (7 tests): the script on a made-up page, the case of two apps on one key (steps 1 and 2), and that every app's line carries its catalogue key and button and is the page's first script.
- **In Chrome:**
  - a clean browser with a Chinese device: the home page and all five apps open in Chinese; with an English device, all in English;
  - English device, 中文 pressed in the wool: Tensor Studio, Structure Studio, step 1, the London map and the home page then open in Chinese;
  - all six pages left open, EN pressed in Tensor Studio: the other five change within 1.5 s; 中文 pressed on the home page: all change back;
  - inside the catalogue, the shell's switch still drives the framed app, and the two keys agree.
- `node tools/check-isolation.mjs`, `node --test` (201 pass), `node tools/build-site.mjs`.
