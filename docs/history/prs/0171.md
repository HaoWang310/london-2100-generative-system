## What changes

A check of every app at phone size (390 × 844, touch) found the layouts and today's features in order, and three faults in the wool bands, all older than today's work:

1. **The map could not be moved with a finger.** It heard the mouse only; two fingers zoomed the whole page.
2. **Only one parcel could be taken.** More need Shift, and a phone has none; a second tap replaced the first.
3. **The folded sheet's two buttons did nothing at steps 16 and 17.** Next at step 16 did not reach step 17, Previous at step 17 did not leave it, and the hand-over waited in the opened sheet, out of sight.

### Now
- **The map** (`index.html`): one finger moves it, two fingers zoom it about the point between them, a tap is a click (an anchor when editing, a parcel at step 17).
- **Step 17 under a finger**: a tap adds a parcel, a second tap on it drops it; Clear empties them. The hint, the step text and the status line speak of taps on a phone or tablet.
- **The folded sheet** (`skin.js`, `flow.js`): Next at step 16 goes on to step 17, Previous at step 17 comes back, and at step 17 Next is the hand-over itself, **Next · Tensor Studio**: white once Tensor Studio can take the parcels, grey until then, with what is missing in the status line.
- **The chain row** (`shared/ui/chain.css`, loaded as `?v=2`): its buttons are 44 px high under a finger, in every app.
- The mouse is unchanged.
