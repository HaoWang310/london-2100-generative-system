Data sources, analysis resolution and georeferencing

Basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors; OpenFreeMap provides map tiles. OSM source

3D building footprints and locations use the same OSM / OpenFreeMap vector tiles. Heights are taken from available height or floor-count attributes; missing heights use estimates from the source. This is not a citywide measured 3D model. Building data fields

Place search: OSM Nominatim returns georeferenced results by name, address or postcode. Requests run only on search submission or Enter. © OpenStreetMap contributors

Terrain: Environment Agency LiDAR Composite DTM 1 m (Open Government Licence). The site caches a terrain overview retrieved on 2026-10-11 and aligned to the same EPSG:3857 extent as the catchment raster. Since v3.21.16, browser Workers generate Web Mercator XYZ tiles locally rather than requesting EA WMS/WMTS while navigating, zooming or tilting. Visible detail depends on the cached overview resolution and is not equivalent to loading individual 1 m DEM pixels. Official data catalogue · Terrain display metadata

Contours: Derived in a browser Worker with marching squares from the locally stored OS Terrain 50 (July 2026) DEM. The standard contour interval is 10 m, with index contours at 50 m. These are derived isolines rather than the original published Ordnance Survey contour vectors, but use its standard 10 m vertical interval. OS Terrain 50 · Local DEM metadata

Drainage: One complete upper Thames 50 m contributing-area raster (0.0025 km² per cell) is kept as the dynamic threshold source. Contributing area is no longer a separate display layer. The terrain-derived drainage network filters this same raster with the selected minimum area; no second network dataset is generated. D8 / depression-filling method · Shared hydrology metadata

Source CRS: EPSG:27700, raster 2801 × 2801, extent E460012–600062 / N110348–250398 (140.05 × 140.05 km). The network is reprojected onto the same EPSG:3857 raster as the catchment layer, following OSM pan and zoom without manual offsets. Georeferencing does not improve the original 50 m analysis resolution. Display uses the full reprojected 2868 × 2875 raster rather than enlarging a 320 × 321 thumbnail; cubic rendering preserves NoData and 70 km clipping.Catchment display metadata

Catchment validation: The full upper Thames domain underwent 50 m DEM hydrological conditioning, D8 flow direction and flow accumulation before clipping to the London 70 km study area. NRFA 39001 (Thames at Kingston) reports 9,948 km², compared with 9,957.1 km² from this model near the station, a relative difference of approximately 0.09%.NRFA 39001

The drainage network represents potential terrain-derived flow pathways, not sewers, measured water levels or the extent of flooding.

Local flood simulation: Terrain uses the same OS Terrain 50 raster reprojected to EPSG:3857 at 50 m resolution. Buildings come from current OSM / OpenFreeMap vector tiles and block runoff through footprints. Decorative water on building bases informs visualization and planning direction only, not the simulated water budget. The browser approximates rainfall-runoff propagation with mass-conserving terrain routing and highlights buildings when footprints or adjacent depths reach 5 cm. This conceptual urban-design comparison must not replace engineering flood modelling with 1 m LiDAR, sewer networks and 2D shallow-water equations.Terrain data metadata

Flood data: © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2025, Open Government Licence v3.0. Rivers / sea RoFRS · Surface water RoFSW · Flood layer metadata

The regional overview was retrieved from the official WMS on 2026-10-11 at 12.5 m sampling in EPSG:27700, summarized into 25 m display cells and aligned to the EPSG:3857 catchment extent. At street scale, official present-day flood-risk tiles are requested at OSM EPSG:3857 coordinates and the higher available class is shown per pixel, without 25 m aggregation. Display cells may be around 1–3 m at typical London street zooms; they do not add modelling accuracy. Both scales share the same legend and 70 km clipping. Blank areas do not imply zero risk. If detailed service is unavailable, the overview remains visible with a notice.
