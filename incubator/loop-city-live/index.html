Loop City live

Loop City 实时应用

Three pages that work together. Start with 01: it opens 02, and 02 opens 03, as you go.

三个页面协同工作。从 01 开始：01 会打开 02，02 再打开 03。

01Mould and cells黏菌与单元 The slime mould over the whole loop. Shift-click up to 10 neighbouring cells, then double-click to send them to 02. 整个环线上的黏菌。按住 Shift 点击最多 10 个相邻单元，然后双击，发送到 02。 02Wool毛线 Opens from 01 with your cells: the wool, the road hierarchy, parcels, capsule stops, and a zoom-in down to every junction. 从 01 带着所选单元打开：毛线、道路等级、地块、胶囊站点，并可放大到每个路口。 03Base for the tensor field张量场底图 Opens from step 16 of 02 with the parcels you took. It saves the site file for the tensor studio. 从 02 的第 16 步带着所选地块打开，保存给张量场工作室使用的用地文件。
