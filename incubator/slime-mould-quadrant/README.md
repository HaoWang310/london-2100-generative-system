# Slime mould · station cells, north-west quadrant · 黏菌模拟 · 西北象限车站单元

The final slime mould model of Loop City on zones 05–06, the north-west quadrant of the loop. Agents leave the five stations: P1, S1, A3, P2 and S8. Their trails thicken into veins along the rail corridor and close into a cell around each station. The run pauses at step 900; the app then finds the cells and can save them.

Loop City 最终版黏菌模型，作用于 05–06 区（环线西北象限）。智能体从五个车站（P1、S1、A3、P2、S8）出发，轨迹沿铁路走廊加粗成脉络，并在每个车站周围围合成单元。模拟在第 900 步暂停，随后识别并可保存这些单元。
