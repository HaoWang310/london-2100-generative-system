生成步骤

说明
How the roads come from the field.
The field holds, at every point, one direction a street should run (the contour, bent by the attractors and the wind). Field · moving draws those directions as drifting streaks; still lines draws them as fixed lines. Each street starts at one of your anchors (or 300 m / 115 m from the last) and simply follows the field until it meets another street: so every street lies on one of those lines. The wind layer is the weather itself; it builds nothing, it only bends the field.

Growth mode

How B grows.
Main streets start at your anchors and grow inward, 5 m at a time, all at once. Each turns toward the field's direction quickly where one direction rules (clarity high) and keeps its heading where the parts cancel. As it grows it puts out side streets, one side then the other, so they meet it as staggered T's: further apart on clear ground, closer where the field is weak. Where the contour and the wind (with the attractors and streams) pull hard in different directions (conflict), a street forks into the two directions: a Y. A street stops where it meets another: square on, a T; at a shallow angle, it merges (a Y); head on, the two become one street. Then the land's edge road puts out side streets where there is room, and any empty ground left is reached from the nearest street. Complete the main streets first (v6.1): before anything branches, contour avenues run from edge to edge, each circus is tied in by up to three main streets, and a main street crosses another as a crossroads; at the end, a long second street running from a main street (or the edge) to another becomes a main street. The X's that remain are main on main.

1 · The field

2 · Wind simulation

说明

From the streets along the contour, the weather year plays month by month (London Luton, hour by hour): each month's winds blow over the site as moving vectors, as often and as strong as they were, and bend the field; the streets regrow in it, the month before fading out. It ends on the whole year. Winds from the south-east round to the west ask for streets along them (air through); from the north-west round to the east, across them (shelter).

3 · Attractors

说明

Add one by hand (v8): double-click on the land for a main attractor, shift + double-click for a secondary one, alt + double-click for one on the edge; right-click an attractor to remove it. This switches to your placement (by hand), keeping the ones already there; your points stay while you change other settings, and come back if you pick another placement and then this one again. Drag an attractor on the map to move it; one on the edge slides along the edge. From the field: where the year's winds cancel, the calmest first, at least 450 m apart (the first two as v6, close together in the centre-east). Near the centre: one at the land's deepest middle; two to four on a ring round it, a fifth of the land's size out, turned along its length. Near the corners: at the land's farthest corners, spread apart, each moved in until it is 150 m from the edge. Your placement: the two main ones where you put them on 21 Oct, north-centre and south-east, about 1 km apart (the secondary ones stay at the calm points). Spread out: each takes its own share of the land (calmer land weighs more), the main ones far apart. On the edge: the first three where you drew them on 21 Oct (north, east, south-west), any more along straight stretches of the edge with deep land behind them, spread along it; the half of the ring field outside the land falls away, leaving semicircles whose radial streets converge on a half circus at the edge road, a square at the gate.

4 · The streets

4b · Mode B, branching

说明

Main streets apart (above) sets how close two main streets may run side by side; second streets apart sets the side streets' spacing: along the field's main direction, second × (0.7 + 0.9 × clarity) × this slider (long blocks on clear ground); across it, second × 0.75 × this slider. Only second streets fork (with the main streets completed first, main streets stay single). A fork needs the contour's and the other forces' street directions at least 35° apart: a shallower fork reads as two streets side by side; where they are square to each other, one grid serves both, so no fork.
