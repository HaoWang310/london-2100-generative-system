# Wool on the mould cells · C12 + C13 · 黏菌单元上的毛线

Two of the slime mould's station cells, C12 (round station P2) and C13, taken step by step to a road network:
- the cells on the vein centrelines;
- the existing roads and towns that cut them, and the soil that splits them again;
- the anchors and the net;
- the wool relaxing live;
- the road hierarchy, read by continuity in seven stages;
- the roads at their real widths with filleted junctions;
- the parcels.

黏菌的两个车站单元 C12（P2 车站周围）与 C13，逐步发展为道路网络：
- 菌脉中心线上的单元；
- 切分单元的现有道路与城镇，以及再次划分单元的土壤；
- 锚点与网；
- 实时松弛的毛线；
- 按连续性分七个阶段读取的道路等级；
- 按真实宽度绘制并带交叉口倒角的道路；
- 地块。

- **Open `index.html`.** It is one file (2.9 MB) with no server.
  打开 `index.html` 即可，单个文件（2.9 MB），无需服务器。
- **Two panels.** The left panel changes the outcome: the anchors, the wool and the road rules. The right panel changes only the look: dark or light, the colours of the threads and roads, a line weight for each road class, the rail, and every layer with its opacity.
  左侧面板改变结果（锚点、毛线、道路规则）；右侧面板只改变外观（深色或浅色、毛线与道路颜色、各级道路线宽、铁路、各图层及其不透明度）。
- **Run the relaxation**, then steps 11 to 13. The run takes about 10–40 s on a laptop.
  按“运行松弛”，再看第 11 至 13 步；笔记本电脑上约 10–40 秒。
- **The road rules:**
  - M is the cell edge.
  - A roads are added one at a time where land is out of reach, never side by side.
  - No new road runs along an existing one.
  - Every loose end is joined to a road.

  道路规则：M 为单元边界；A 级道路逐条加在覆盖不到的土地最多之处，且彼此不平行；新道路不沿现有道路布置；每个断头都连接到道路。
- **English | 中文** switch at the top left of the map.
  地图左上角可切换中英文。
